## 1. How many days of PTO do I get?

> As a full-time employee you get *28 days of PTO per calendar year*. You also get the public holidays of the country you're employed in, on top of the 28. If you're part-time, your allowance is pro-rated. PTO accrues at 2.33 days per month, but you can take days before they've accrued. The company shutdown from *24 December to 1 January* doesn't count against your PTO, and you can carry up to *5 unused days* into the next year as long as you use them by 31 March.

## 2. I'm going to the autumn retreat, how do I book my flight and when?

> Book your flight in *TravelPerk*, signed in with your Orbit Google account, not on a personal card. Use the trip label `retreat-2026-autumn` and book only your flights there, because Ops has already booked the retreat venue. Do it *at least 6 weeks before* the retreat. Bookings made closer than that need Ops approval and usually cost 30–50% more. Fly economy unless the flight is *longer than 8 hours*, in which case premium economy or business is allowed. If you need a visa, ask for an invitation letter in #ops-travel at least 8 weeks ahead. The knowledge base doesn't give the actual autumn retreat dates, so check those with #ops-travel to work out your deadline.

Sources: travel-booking.md · cost $0.0308 · 3 turns · 8.4s

## 3. What's the hotel cap for a conference in London?

> For a conference in London, the hotel cap is *USD 300 per night*. The standard cap is USD 220 per night, and London gets the higher rate along with San Francisco and New York. Book the hotel through *TravelPerk* in the same trip as your flights, using the trip label `conf-<event name>`.

Sources: travel-booking.md · cost $0.0263 · 3 turns · 5.6s

## 4. Can I spend $400 on a standing desk for my home office?

> Yes. Home-office equipment (desk, chair, monitor) is covered up to *USD 500 per year* with no pre-approval needed, so a $400 standing desk fits as long as you haven't already spent more than $100 of that allowance this year. Submit it in Expensify with the receipt (needed for anything over USD 25) within *30 days* of buying it. After that, it needs director approval. If the desk would take you over $500 for the year, get written approval from your manager *before* you buy it.

Sources: expense-policy.md · cost $0.0275 · 3 turns · 6.4s
