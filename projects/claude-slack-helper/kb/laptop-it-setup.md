# Laptop and IT setup

**Owner:** IT (#it-help) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10

## Standard laptop

New hires receive a **MacBook Pro 14-inch, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD**. Researchers who need more memory can request the **64 GB** model with a one-line justification in #it-help. Linux laptops (Framework 13) are available on request; Windows is not supported.

Laptops are replaced every **3 years** or when repair costs exceed 40% of a new one.

## First-day checklist

1. Sign in with your `@orbit-research.org` Google account.
2. Install the MDM profile from the email titled "Welcome to Orbit IT". This enables disk encryption and remote wipe; it does **not** read your files or browsing.
3. Set up 1Password (our password manager). Your invite arrives on day one.
4. Enable two-factor authentication on Google, GitHub and Slack. Hardware keys (YubiKey 5C) are free from IT; ask in #it-help.
5. Join Slack, GitHub (`orbit-research` org) and Notion.

## Software

Pre-approved and free to install: VS Code, Cursor, Docker Desktop, Zoom, Slack, Notion, 1Password, Tailscale. Anything else under **USD 50 per month** is covered by the expense policy; above that, ask IT.

## Support

Post in **#it-help** for anything laptop or account related. Response time target is **4 working hours**; urgent lockouts are usually handled within 1 hour during European and US-East working hours.

## Lost or stolen laptop

Report it in #it-help **immediately** (within 24 hours at most). IT will trigger a remote wipe. You will not be charged for a lost laptop, but a second loss within two years is reviewed by your manager.

## Leaving Orbit

Return the laptop within 14 days of your last day; IT sends a prepaid shipping label.
