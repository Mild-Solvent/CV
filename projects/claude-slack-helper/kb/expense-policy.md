# Expense policy

**Owner:** Finance (#finance) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09

Orbit Research reimburses reasonable work expenses. Use Expensify; attach a receipt for anything over **USD 25**. Submit within **30 days** of the purchase or it needs director approval.

## Limits that do not need pre-approval

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meals while travelling | USD 60 per day |
| Team meal you host (per person) | USD 40 |
| Books, papers, courses | USD 300 per year |
| Software subscriptions | USD 50 per month per tool |
| Home-office equipment | USD 500 per year (desk, chair, monitor) |
| Conference tickets | USD 1,500 per event |

Anything above these limits needs written approval from your manager **before** you spend. Post in #finance if you are unsure.

## What we do not reimburse

- Alcohol, except at an official team event
- Fines, parking tickets, lost-item replacements
- Upgrades to business class (see the travel doc for the one exception on flights over 8 hours)
- Personal phone plans (we pay a flat **USD 30 per month** stipend instead, added to payroll)

## Reimbursement timing

Approved reports are paid on the **15th and the last working day** of each month. If a report is still "pending" after 10 working days, ping the Finance lead in #finance.

## Company card

Managers and the ops team have company cards. Card purchases still need a receipt in Expensify within 30 days; the card is not a way around the limits above.
