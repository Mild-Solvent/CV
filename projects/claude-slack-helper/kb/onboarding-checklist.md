# Onboarding checklist for new hires

**Owner:** People team (#people-ops) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09

Welcome to Orbit Research. This is what your first weeks look like. Your onboarding buddy walks you through it; your manager owns it.

## Before day one

- Sign your contract in Humaans and complete the right-to-work check.
- Receive your laptop (shipped to arrive 2 working days before you start).
- Fill in the "About me" form so the team knows your time zone and pronouns.

## Day one

- 30-minute call with the People team: payroll, benefits, PTO tool.
- Set up your laptop (see the IT doc). Enable two-factor authentication everywhere.
- Join Slack. Introduce yourself in #general. Join #ai-tools, #it-help, #people-ops and your team channel.
- Meet your onboarding buddy.

## Week one

- Read the research strategy doc and the data handling rules.
- 1:1 with your manager to agree your **first 30-day goals**.
- Have at least **three intro calls** with people outside your team (your buddy suggests who).
- Claim your Claude Team seat and GitHub Copilot seat (automatic; check your email).

## First month

- Ship something small and visible: a doc, an analysis, a pull request.
- 30-day check-in with your manager and the People team.
- Book travel for the next retreat if it is within 3 months.

## Probation

Probation is **3 months**. You get written feedback at the 30-day and 90-day check-ins. There are no surprises: if something is not working, you hear it at the 30-day point.

## Buddy duties

Buddies are volunteers from another team. They have a 15-minute check-in with you **twice a week for the first month**, then weekly until month three.
