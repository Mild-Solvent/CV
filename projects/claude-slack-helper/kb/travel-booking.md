# Booking travel for retreats and conferences

**Owner:** Ops (#ops-travel) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-08

Orbit Research holds **two all-hands retreats per year** (spring and autumn, usually five nights). Travel for retreats and conferences is booked through our agency, **TravelPerk**, not on personal cards.

## How to book

1. Log in to TravelPerk with your Orbit Google account.
2. Pick a trip label: `retreat-2026-autumn` or `conf-<event name>`.
3. Book flights and hotel in one trip so Finance sees the total.
4. Book retreat travel **at least 6 weeks before** the retreat. Bookings inside 6 weeks need Ops approval and usually cost 30–50% more.

## Flights

- Economy by default. **Premium economy or business is allowed on flights longer than 8 hours** of scheduled flight time; this is the only class upgrade we pay for.
- Flexible fares are fine if the price difference is under USD 150.
- You may keep airline miles.

## Hotels

- The retreat venue is pre-booked by Ops; you only book travel to it.
- For conferences, hotel cap is **USD 220 per night** (USD 300 in San Francisco, New York and London).

## Ground transport

Taxis and ride-shares to and from airports are reimbursable. Rental cars need Ops approval.

## Visas and insurance

Orbit pays visa fees and provides travel insurance through TravelPerk automatically. Request a visa invitation letter in #ops-travel at least 8 weeks ahead.

## Travel days

If you travel on a weekend for a retreat, you may take **one day in lieu** within the following month. Log it as "Travel day" in the PTO tool.
