# Paid time off (PTO) policy

**Owner:** People team (#people-ops) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07

## Allowance

Every full-time employee gets **28 days of PTO per calendar year**, plus the public holidays of the country you are employed in. Part-time staff get a pro-rated amount. The allowance accrues monthly (2.33 days per month) but you can take days before they accrue.

## Carry-over

You can carry over **up to 5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** or they expire. We do not pay out unused days except when you leave.

## How to request

1. Open the PTO tool (Humaans) and submit the dates.
2. Your manager approves in Humaans. Requests for **more than 10 consecutive working days need approval at least 4 weeks in advance**; shorter requests need 1 week notice.
3. Add it to your calendar and set your Slack status.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and not capped. Tell your manager by the start of the working day. After **5 consecutive sick days** we ask for a doctor's note.

## Other leave

- **Parental leave:** 16 weeks fully paid for all parents.
- **Bereavement:** 5 days paid.
- **Jury duty / civic duty:** paid, no limit.
- **Retreat travel days:** see the travel doc.

## Company shutdown

Orbit closes between **24 December and 1 January**. Those days do **not** come out of your PTO allowance.

## Questions

Ask in #people-ops. Anything about contracts or local law goes to the People lead directly, not in a public channel.
